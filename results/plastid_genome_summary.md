## Notable Features

Examples of protein-coding genes include **psaA, psbA, atpA, petA, rbcL, rpoB, rpl2,** and **matK**.

The genome also contains intron-containing genes, including **rps16, atpF, rpoC1, ycf3, clpP, petB, petD,** and **ndhA**. The **rps12** gene has a notable trans-spliced organization.

## Interpretation

The Galaxy results show that the FASTA contains one continuous 153,119-bp sequence with no gaps or ambiguous N bases. Together with the NCBI annotation identifying the sequence as a complete circular chloroplast genome, these results support the characterization of NC_037879.1 as a complete *Paeonia suffruticosa* plastid genome.

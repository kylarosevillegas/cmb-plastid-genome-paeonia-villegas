## Summary

The annotated *Paeonia suffruticosa* chloroplast genome contains genes involved in photosynthesis, electron transport, ATP synthesis, carbon fixation, transcription, translation, and RNA processing. The presence of multiple conserved plastid gene groups and the characteristic IR regions supports the identification of this sequence as a complete chloroplast genome.
